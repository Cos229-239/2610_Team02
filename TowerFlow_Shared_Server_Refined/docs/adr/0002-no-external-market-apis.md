# No external price feeds or unconsented advertising

**Status: accepted for prototype.** Azure Flow may calculate holdings from manually supplied historical observations but must not present them as current quotes, connect to a brokerage or intercept phone conversations for advertising. A no-external-API boundary reduces network and privacy risk but prevents independent real-time market awareness; a future external data source requires a new approval and provenance/freshness contract. Advertising will use explicitly authorized inputs and approved distribution channels.
